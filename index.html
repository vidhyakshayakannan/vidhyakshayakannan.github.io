Vidhyakshaya Kannan

I am a master’s student in Symbolic Systems at Stanford University, where I began in Autumn 2026. I am a teaching assistant for CS224V: Agentic AI, taught by Professor Monica Lam.

I work on NLP and language model evaluation with the Georgia Tech Financial Services Innovation Lab, supervised by Dr. Sudheer Chava. I have also studied cognitive mechanisms of temporal attention with Dr. Jamshed Bharucha.

I earned my B.Tech. in Computing and Data Science, with a minor in English Literature, at Sai University. As a Global UGRAD Scholar, I spent Autumn 2024 at New England College on a fully funded exchange, as one of five scholars selected from India.

During my undergraduate studies, I was fortunate to be advised by Dr. Ram Prasad Krishnamoorthy and Dr. Krithi Ramamritham.
